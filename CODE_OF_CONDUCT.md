# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented. Debate product decisions and technical trade-offs without attacking people. Assume good intent, acknowledge uncertainty, and make room for contributors with different levels of experience.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving reproducible feedback;
- explaining why a change helps learners or maintainers;
- reviewing code and UX with concrete evidence;
- crediting prior work and third-party sources;
- protecting private learner data and project credentials.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, or knowingly submitting unsafe or plagiarized material.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this standard. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the GitHub profile contact method. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
