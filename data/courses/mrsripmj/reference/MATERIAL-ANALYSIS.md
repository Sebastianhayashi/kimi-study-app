# 教材内容分析

## 教材基本信息

- **书名**：英语（三年级起点）四年级上册
- **出版社**：福建教育出版社
- **主编**：陈维振
- **副主编**：张祥和、黄远振
- **编写依据**：义务教育英语课程标准（2011年版）
- **总页数**：44 页（扫描版）

## 内容单元划分

### Unit 1 Our Classroom（教室）

**核心词汇**：classroom, blackboard, desk, chair, tiger, read, after, look at
**核心句型**：
- I'm in Class..., Grade...
- Look at the blackboard.
- Now look at the blackboard. Read after me.
**语音**：字母组合 ar /ɑː/（car, park, star）
**歌曲歌谣**：In the Classroom, My Classroom

### Unit 2 Our School（学校）

**核心词汇**：school, playground, library, computer room, big, cousin, from, that
**核心句型**：
- Which class are you in?
- I'm in Class..., Grade...
- This is our computer room / library.
**语音**：字母组合 ir /ɜː/（skirt, girl）
**歌曲歌谣**：My Happy School, In the Playground

### Unit 3 Numbers and Animals（数字与动物）

**核心词汇**：thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, twenty, cow, horse, sheep, pig, farm, clever, how much
**核心句型**：
- How much is ... and ...?
- It's ...
- I have ... cows / horses / sheep / pigs.
**语音**：字母组合 th /θ/（three, thirty, mouth, birthday, father, mother, brother）
**歌曲歌谣**：Old MacDonald Has a Farm, Twenty

### Unit 4 Numbers and Time（数字与时间）

**核心词汇**：thirty, forty, fifty, sixty, slow, sleep, begin
**核心句型**：
- What's the time?
- It's ... o'clock.
- It's nine thirty / half past nine.
**语音**：字母组合 oy /ɔɪ/（boy, toy）
**歌曲歌谣**：What's the Time?, Come On

### Unit 5 Daily Activities（日常活动）

**核心词汇**：get up, go to school, have lunch, go home, go to bed, bus, full, get into, car, when, do, at, breakfast, lunch, bed, to
**核心句型**：
- When do you ...?
- I ... at ... a.m. / p.m.
- It's time to go to school.
**语音**：字母组合 ow /əʊ/（window, yellow）
**歌曲歌谣**：It's Time to Go to School, Tick, Tock

### Unit 6 Meals（三餐）

**核心词汇**：milk, bread, tomato, them, welcome, dinner, beef, potato, vegetable, some
**核心句型**：
- What's for breakfast / lunch / dinner?
- Do you like ...?
- Yes, I do. / No, thank you.
- Have some ..., please.
**语音**：字母组合 ea /e/（head, bread, sweater）
**歌曲歌谣**：What's for Dinner?, Cooking

### Unit 7 Christmas（圣诞节）

**核心词汇**：Christmas, robot, tree, email, merry, present, card
**核心句型**：
- What present do you want?
- I want a ...
- Merry Christmas!
**语音**：字母组合 ow /əʊ/（window, yellow）重复
**歌曲歌谣**：A Merry Christmas, Jingle Bells

### Unit 8 The Spring Festival（春节）

**核心词汇**：spring, festival, the Spring Festival, Chinese, spend, we, make, dumpling, family, watch, TV, get, money, fair, friend, say
**核心句型**：
- How do you spend the Spring Festival?
- We make dumplings.
- We watch the Spring Festival Party on TV.
- Do you ...? Yes, we do.
**语音**：字母组合 ay /eɪ/（play, email）
**歌曲歌谣**：To the Fair, Draw and Color
