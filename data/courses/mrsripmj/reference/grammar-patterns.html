《英语 四年级上册》核心句型与语法点

按单元整理的重点句型，便于口语输出和写作参考

Unit 1 Our Classroom
句型含义例句
I'm in Class ..., Grade ...我在……年级……班I'm in Class One, Grade Four.
Look at the blackboard.看黑板。—
Read after me.跟着我读。—
注意：英语说班级要先说 Class，再说 Grade；Class 和 Grade 与数字连用时首字母大写。
Unit 2 Our School
句型含义例句
Which class are you in?你在哪个班？I'm in Class Two, Grade Four.
This is our ...这是我们的……This is our computer room.
Unit 3 Numbers and Animals
句型含义例句
How much is ... and ...?……加……等于多少？How much is thirteen and seven?
It's ...等于……It's twenty.
I have ...我有……I have two horses.
Unit 4 Numbers and Time
句型含义例句
What's the time?几点了？—
It's ... o'clock.……点了。It's ten o'clock.
It's half past ...……点半了。It's half past nine.
Unit 5 Daily Activities
句型含义例句
When do you ...?你什么时候……？When do you get up?
I ... at ...我……在……点。I get up at 6:30 a.m.
It's time to ...到……的时间了。It's time to go to school.
Unit 6 Meals
句型含义例句
What's for breakfast / lunch / dinner?早/午/晚餐吃什么？—
Do you like ...?你喜欢……吗？Do you like vegetables?
Yes, I do. / No, thank you.是的，我喜欢。/ 不用了，谢谢。—
Have some ..., please.请吃点/喝点……Have some beef, please.
Unit 7 Christmas
句型含义例句
What present do you want?你想要什么礼物？I want a computer.
Merry Christmas!圣诞快乐！—
Unit 8 The Spring Festival
句型含义例句
How do you spend the Spring Festival?你们怎么过春节？We make dumplings.
Do you ...?你们……吗？Do you watch TV after dinner?
Yes, we do.是的，我们……—
