二、口头小作文示范：Christmas

Christmas is a happy festival. We put a Christmas tree in our house. Then we put lights and stars on the tree. Mom and Dad put presents under the tree. On Christmas Day we open the presents. We wish each other a merry Christmas and a happy New Year.

来源：source:book#page=68

三、口头小作文示范：The Spring Festival

The Spring Festival is Chinese New Year. We make rice cakes and dumplings. We have a big family dinner. After dinner, we watch the Spring Festival Party on TV. We say Happy New Year to our friends. Children can get some New Year's money. It's a happy festival, too.

来源：source:book#page=69

四、综合练习

练习 1：快速反应

看到中文，立刻说出英文：

  • 四年级一班 → Class One, Grade Four
  • 这是我们的图书馆 → This is our library.
  • 几点了？ → What's the time?
  • 你什么时候上学？ → When do you go to school?
  • 晚饭吃什么？ → What's for dinner?
  • 你想要什么礼物？ → What present do you want?
练习 2：数字大挑战

快速读出并写出英文：

  1. 15 → __________
  2. 20 → __________
  3. 36 → __________
  4. 44 → __________
  5. 50 → __________
练习 3：时间表达

写出下列时间的英文：

  1. 6:30 a.m. → __________
  2. 7:15 a.m. → __________
  3. 12:00 → __________
  4. 9:00 p.m. → __________
练习 4：节日对比

比较 Christmas 和 The Spring Festival，各写出 3 个关键词：

  • Christmas: Christmas tree, presents, merry
  • The Spring Festival: __________, __________, __________
