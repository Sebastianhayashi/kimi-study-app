Lesson 0008 · Meals

谈论一日三餐与食物 · 预计 12 分钟

本课目标：能说出常见食物名称，能用 What's for …? 和 Do you like …? 谈论三餐。

一、复习：日常活动时间

A: When do you have breakfast?
B: I have breakfast at 7:00 a.m.

注意：上节课我们学了 have breakfast，今天我们会学更多和“吃”有关的表达。

四、重点句型

句型 1：询问三餐吃什么

A: What's for breakfast, Mom?
B: Milk, bread, eggs and tomatoes.
A: What's for dinner?
B: Rice and beef.

句型 2：请别人吃/喝某物

A: Have some potatoes, please.
B: Thank you. / No, thank you.

句型 3：询问喜好

A: Do you like vegetables?
B: Yes, I do.

六、练一练

练习 1：分类游戏

把下面的食物按三餐分类（答案不唯一）：

  • breakfast: bread, milk, eggs, __________
  • lunch: noodles, beef, __________
  • dinner: rice, fish, vegetables, __________
练习 2：问答配对

把问题和答案连起来：

  • What's for breakfast? → Milk, bread and eggs.
  • Have some beef, please. → __________
  • Do you like vegetables? → __________
  • What's for dinner? → __________
练习 3：替换对话

用不同食物替换，和同伴练习：

A: Do you like __________?
B: Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

七、本课小挑战

🍽️
请完成这个任务：写一段你和家人的早餐或晚餐对话。
例如：A: What's for breakfast, Mom? B: Bread, milk and eggs. A: Do you like tomatoes? B: Yes, I do. A: Have some milk, please. B: Thank you.
把对话发给我，至少包含 What's for …?、Do you like …? 和 Have some …, please. 三个句型。
