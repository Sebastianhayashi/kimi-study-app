本课目标：能用 There is / There are 和 I can see ... 描述教室里有什么，并能演唱课本歌曲。

三、重点句型

句型 1：某地有某物

There's a blackboard on the wall.
There are chairs and there are windows in the classroom.

小贴士：后面跟“一个”用 There is（缩写 There's），跟“多个”用 There are。

句型 2：我能看到……

In my classroom, I can see a blackboard.
I can see desks and chairs for you and for me.

来源：source:book#page=6

四、歌曲：In the Classroom / My Classroom

五、练一练

练习 1：填空唱一唱

先读一遍歌词，然后遮住课本，试着补全下面的句子：

  1. In the classroom, there's a __________ on the wall.
  2. There are __________ and there are windows in the classroom.
  3. In my classroom, I can see desks and __________.
练习 2：There is / There are 选择

请用正确的句型开头：

  1. __________ a teacher in the classroom.
  2. __________ many desks in the classroom.
  3. __________ two windows on the wall.
  4. __________ a blackboard on the wall.
练习 3：看一看，说一说

环顾你的真实教室或房间，用下面的句型说 4 句话：

  • In my classroom, I can see ...
  • There's a / an ... on / in ...
  • There are some ... in ...

六、本课小挑战

📝
请完成这个任务：用 3–5 句话描述你的教室或房间。
例如：In my classroom, I can see a blackboard. There are many desks and chairs. There's a door and two windows. I like my classroom.
把你说的话用文字发给我，我会帮你检查 there is / there are 用得对不对。
