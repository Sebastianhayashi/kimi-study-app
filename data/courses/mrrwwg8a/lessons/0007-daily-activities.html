Lesson 0007 · Daily Activities

描述一天的活动安排 · 预计 15 分钟

本课目标：能用英语说出自己一天中主要活动的时间，并能询问他人的日常安排。

一、复习：时间与数字

快速说出下面的时间：

6:30 → six thirty
7:15 → seven fifteen
12:00 → twelve o'clock
4:30 → four thirty

你还记得上节课的加法吗？How much is twenty and sixteen? It's thirty-six.

三、重点句型

句型 1：询问和回答做某事的时间

A: When do you go to school?
B: I go to school at 7:15 a.m.

句型 2：描述他人的一天（注意第三人称用 has / goes）

Lily gets up at 6:30 a.m.
She has breakfast at 6:45 a.m.
She goes to school at 7:15 a.m.
She has lunch at 12:00.
She goes home at 4:30 p.m.
She goes to bed at 9:00 p.m.

句型 3：该做某事了

It's time to get up.
It's time to go to school.
Hurry up! It's time to go to school.

五、练一练

练习 1：时间与活动配对

把左边的活动和右边的时间连起来：

  • get up → 6:30 a.m.
  • have breakfast → 6:45 a.m.
  • go to school → 7:15 a.m.
  • have lunch → 12:00
  • go home → 4:30 p.m.
  • go to bed → 9:00 p.m.
练习 2：问答接龙

仿照例句，根据时间完成问答：

  1. 6:30 a.m. / get up → When do you get up? I get up at 6:30 a.m.
  2. 7:00 a.m. / go to school → __________
  3. 12:00 / have lunch → __________
  4. 9:00 p.m. / go to bed → __________
练习 3：第三人称转换

把 “I …” 改成 “She / He …”：

  1. I get up at 6:30. → She __________ at 6:30.
  2. I have breakfast at 7:00. → He __________ at 7:00.
  3. I go to school at 7:20. → She __________ at 7:20.
