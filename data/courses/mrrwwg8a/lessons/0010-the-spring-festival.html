Lesson 0010 · The Spring Festival

春节习俗与祝福 · 预计 12 分钟

本课目标：能用英语介绍春节的习俗，能询问和回答“怎么过春节”。

一、复习：圣诞节

A: What present do you want?
B: I want a robot.
A: Merry Christmas!

今天我们来学习中国最重要的传统节日——春节。

三、重点句型

句型 1：询问和回答“怎么过春节”

A: What's the Spring Festival?
B: It's Chinese New Year.
A: How do you spend the Spring Festival?
B: We make rice cakes. We make dumplings.
B: We have a family dinner.
B: We watch the Spring Festival Party on TV.
B: We get some New Year's money.
B: We go to see our friends. We go to the fair, too.

句型 2：一般疑问句

A: Do you watch TV after dinner?
B: Yes. We watch the Spring Festival Party on TV.
A: Do you get any presents?
B: Yes, we do. We get some New Year's money.

五、练一练

练习 1：习俗配对

把活动和图片含义连起来：

  • make rice cakes → 做年糕
  • make dumplings → 包饺子
  • have a family dinner → 吃团圆饭
  • watch the Spring Festival Party on TV → 看春晚
  • get some New Year's money → 收压岁钱
  • go to the fair → 逛庙会
练习 2：问答练习

根据提示完成对话：

  1. A: How do you spend the Spring Festival? B: We __________.（包饺子）
  2. A: Do you have a family dinner? B: __________.（是的）
  3. A: Do you get any New Year's money? B: __________.
  4. A: What's the Spring Festival? B: __________.
练习 3：说祝福语

春节期间，人们会说：

Happy New Year!
Say Happy New Year to your friends.

你还知道哪些春节祝福语？试着用英语说。
